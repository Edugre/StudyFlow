## Gaps and team decisions

- Authentication is a high-priority system prerequisite in the Google Doc but has no Trello card. All stories inherit authenticated ownership requirements even when a card says Dependencies: None. Add a team-owned breakdown before exposing CRUD.
- CM-10 is in This Week but depends on AT-01 and SP-01, both in Product Backlog. Prepare association boundaries now; discuss rescheduling or splitting its integration work rather than treating it as complete.
- The Google Doc's five-sprint plan is explicitly proposed and focuses on analysis, wireframes, test derivation and team review. Trello has story cards rather than separate design/auth/scaffold tasks. Scaffolding alone does not satisfy that definition of done.
- All 50 stories have cards. The Doc's broad initial backlog still lists AT-05/07/08, SP-03/07/08 and RN-03/05/06/07 as needing breakdown; Trello already contains their story cards. Detailed technical subtasks and unassigned priorities remain open.
- No stack or architecture is documented in the sources. This scaffold's decision is local and provisional; record any approved team decision in Drive later through the normal team process.
- Semester vocabulary/current-semester selection, timezone/daylight-saving behavior, duplicate course rules, field limits, and course deletion with linked assignments/sessions/goals are unspecified.
- Notification delivery channel, supported advance intervals, hosting/database backups, auth/session implementation and measurable performance targets remain unspecified. Do not invent these in later feature work.
- Location and notes are documented optional course fields and part of CM-09, but have no dedicated task codes. Keep their edits within CM-06 pending team review.

2. CM-01 adds validation, owner-scoped SQLite adapter, service, authenticated API and add-course UI. Prove nonblank validation, single saved record, reopening and other-account denial.
3. CM-02/03/04/05 add code, instructor, meetings and semester independently after CM-01. Test exact values, per-course isolation and invalid schedule rejection.
4. CM-09 (CM-01 dependency) and CM-08 (CM-01 + CM-05) implement details and semester-filtered list with unset/empty states. They can proceed alongside independent field cards when their dependencies exist.
5. CM-06/07 depend on CM-01; implement editing/cancel and confirmed deletion/cancel. Agree on linked-record deletion policy before integration. CRUD services can support these later without completing the cards during CM-01.
6. CM-10 integrates after CM-01 + AT-01 + SP-01; coordinate with AT-02 and SP-02, which also define course association behavior.
